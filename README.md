### My Projects 🚀

#### 🤖 Neotic — Visual AI Reasoning & RAG Platform

An explainable AI platform that transforms LLM reasoning into interactive DAG visualizations while supporting context-aware document querying through a Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline.

**Tech:** `Python` `FastAPI` `Next.js` `LangChain` `React Flow` `Firebase` `RAG`

---

#### 💼 CareerCraft — AI-Driven Career Recommendation Platform

An AI-powered career platform that generates personalized learning roadmaps, skill assessments, resumes, mock interviews and role-based job recommendations.

**Tech:** `React` `Vite` `Node.js` `Google Gemini API` `RapidAPI`

---

#### 🛣️ Road Damage Detection & Geospatial Monitoring

An end-to-end computer vision system that detects potholes and road cracks from dashcam footage and maps detected hazards using synchronized GPS information.

**Tech:** `Python` `YOLOv8` `OpenCV` `Streamlit` `PyDeck` `GPS`
